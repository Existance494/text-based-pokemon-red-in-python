# Instructions  

  You are going to be creating a fighting game.  The player will continue to fight opponents until their health points are at 0.  For each fight, the game should display which enemy type (or name of the enemy) the player is fighting.  The player should receive prompts for what kind of action they would like to make.  Based off of the action, the opponent will either lose health points or not.  After the opponent's health reaches 0, the player will move on to the next opponent.  If neither the player or the opponent wins after 5 rounds, then the one with the most health points wins.

  Your game is required to have at least 3 opponents.  The damage should be calculated with a random number generator.  Whether you want to make the random generator determine if the attack is a critical hit, miss, or just how much damage is dealt is up to you.  It is up to you if the player's health points should replenish itself after each fight or not.  You are welcome to determine damage output of the characters on your own.  Just make sure that I am able to get an opportunity to check each facet of the game (don't make things to strong or too weak).  If you would like to include items in the game or equipment that is up to you.  If you would like to incorporate random rewards for each/some battles, using a random number genarator is useful.  The game should ask if you would like to play again after winning all of the matches or losing.  If the player chooses yes, then the game should restart.

  You are required to use loops/recursion where appropriate, and you should use functions where useful as well.  If you do not use loops/recursion appropriatey, you will lose 20 points off of the project (This unit is on iteration).  If you do not have any loops/recursion, then your project will receive the minimum score without being ran.  Likewise, if your project does not run, you will receive the minimum score as well.  Be sure to include comments to clarify what certain blocks of code are meant to do.
